The person behind Math Revision

Pete Bromfield

Former Head of Maths with IB examining experience. Former Head of Sixth Form at two schools and former IB Diploma Programme Coordinator; now Director of Studies & Enrichment at an IB Continuum school in Europe.

Pete writes or reviews every question, mark scheme and worked answer on the four Math Revision sites. He built them to give students targeted, exam-style practice on exactly the topics they need, with feedback that shows where the marks go.

CBSE experience: Pete has served on a Council of International Schools (CIS) accreditation evaluation team at a CBSE school, reviewing how teaching and assessment work in practice.

Pete’s examining is his personal experience. Math Revision is independent: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the IB, Pearson Edexcel, Cambridge, AQA, OCR or CBSE.
