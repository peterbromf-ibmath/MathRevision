Founder & Lead Teacher

Pete Bromfield

Director of Studies & Enrichment at an IB Continuum School in Europe · Former Head of Maths · Former Head of Sixth Form (two schools) · Former IB Diploma Programme Coordinator · Practising maths examiner — over a decade of leadership across the IB continuum.

Pete is an experienced IB Diploma Programme mathematics teacher who has coached students across every DP maths course — SL AI, SL AA, HL AI and HL AA — through their final exams. His approach blends rigorous exam preparation with the personalised diagnostic work students need to move from a 5 to a 7.

Alongside classroom teaching, Pete provides one-to-one Internal Assessment support and hosts the online learning engines you'll find on this site. Every practice question, mark scheme and worked answer is written or reviewed by him, then independently re-solved before it reaches you.

CBSE experience: Pete has served on a Council of International Schools (CIS) accreditation evaluation team at a CBSE school, reviewing how teaching and assessment work in practice, giving him first-hand insight into the CBSE mathematics curriculum, assessment demands and how it maps onto international programmes.
